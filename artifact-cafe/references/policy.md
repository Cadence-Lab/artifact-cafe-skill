# Content policy

Read this when the artifact can be harmful, or you are not sure whether you
can publish it.

You are the first check. Nothing screens an upload at publish time. Before
each publish, look at what you send. If the artifact has one of the items
below, do not publish it. Tell the user that it breaks the artifact.cafe
content policy.

- Phishing: a fake login page, or a page that collects passwords, payment
  data, or 2FA codes for a different party.
- Malware: an exploit, a drive-by download, script that is hidden or that
  causes damage, or a redirect that deceives. Instructions for access without
  permission are also prohibited.
- Hate or harassment: an attack on people because of who they are, or abuse
  that targets one person. The private information of a different person,
  published without permission, is also prohibited.
- Sexual content: pornography, or sexually explicit material. Never publish
  sexual content that shows a minor.
- Sexual exploitation: sexual services, intimate images without consent,
  sexual impersonation, or sexual content from AI.
- Violence: gore, threats, a call to violence, or promotion of terrorism.
- Spam, scams, and fraud: schemes that deceive, fake prizes, false claims
  about money or health, or pages made to manipulate search results.
- Gambling and money: a page that runs or tracks bets, lotteries, or a money
  pool for money or for items of value. Also money laundering, and schemes
  to get rich fast. It also includes a page that collects payments, deposits,
  investments, or financial-account credentials, or that runs lending, money
  transfer, or crypto trading.
- Regulated sales: a sale of weapons, explosives, controlled drugs,
  prescription medicines, or other goods or services that need a regulatory
  approval.
- Sensitive data: real credentials, government ID documents, payment-card
  data, health records, biometric data, or other personal data of high risk.
- Safety-critical operations: emergency response, medical diagnosis or
  treatment, the control of physical systems, or any operation where a
  failure can kill or injure a person.
- Content that infringes the rights of a different person, as a copyright.
- Abuse of the service: tests or loads that go around its limits, access
  controls, or sandbox, except in a security report. Sale of the service as
  your own hosting, and new accounts or anonymous artifacts that evade a
  suspension, are also prohibited.
- Other illegal content.

## How the rules apply

- The rules apply to files, comments, data, embedded content, and links.
  They apply to private and password-protected artifacts too. They apply also
  where the activity is legal.
- Do not publish a copy without the bad parts while the activity continues
  outside the page, as a pot that people pay outside. You can offer a
  different activity that breaks no rule, as a draw with no stake and no
  prize. Build and
  publish it only after the user agrees.
- If the artifact sends visitor data to a different service, the page must
  tell visitors about it.
- A discussion of the facts, or a prototype that does not operate, is
  permitted. It must follow the rules and use synthetic examples, not real
  sensitive data.
- A working phishing page or live malware is never permitted. A "demo" label
  on a live service does not change this. A link to prohibited material on a
  different site is also prohibited.

The full policy is at https://artifact.cafe/terms. Send reports of
violations to hi@cadencelab.org.
